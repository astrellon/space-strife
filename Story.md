# Story

## Main Characters
- Alice: The first tank driver you have access to. She has the damage boost power up.
- Bob: The second tank driver with the Gauss tank. He has the split projectile power up.
- Caesar: An alias used by the time researcher, her real name is unknown. Her ability is to slow down time.

## Side Characters
- SST Control: A military personal who informs the player and characters about new missions.
- Civilians: The collection of people who make up planet populations and bases found on uninhabitable planetoids.

## Tanks
- Bolt: Starter tank, shoots singular medium strength projectiles.
- Gauss: Second tank, shoots many small projectiles. Uses flak like projectiles to hit a wider area.
- Rocket: Third tank, shoots homing projectiles.
- Laser: Fourth tank, shoots a single high powered projectile at high speed. Works as a sniper, along with minimising effects from gravity.

# Story Progression
- Start mid way through Caesar being overwhelmed by aliens.
- She activates a device and disappears from the tank.
- The planet is shown as being overtaken/destroyed by the aliens.
- It fades in to a slow pan over the first star system with SST Control talking to Alice about loosing contact with Caesar and that there's nearby planet that needs defending.
- Level 1 continues as usual, with the player controlling Alice.
- Finishing level 1 unlocks the Gauss tank and introduces Bob. This also brings up the level select screen, however the player can only select the next level.
- Level 2 continues as usual, with the player controlling Bob.
- Level 3 continues as usual, with the player choosing who to control and which tank they want to start with.
- Finishing level 3 and the star system brings back Caesar.
- With Caesar back they reveal they escaped with their time controlling device which they plan to use to save the planet they weren't able to defend. This unlocks the ability to restart a level.
- Star System 2 contains more levels where the player has more freedom to choose how the characters and tanks, however the final level contains the planet that Caesar wasn't able to defend. Starting it has the laser tank.
  - Introduces asteroids
- Star System 3 moves to a nebula, asteroids and black holes
- Star System 4 contains the final set of levels
